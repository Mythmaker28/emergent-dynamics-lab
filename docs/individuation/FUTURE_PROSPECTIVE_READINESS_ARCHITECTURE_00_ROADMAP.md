## The distance, stated plainly

The program's destination is **Stage D followed by Stage E**. Stage D — the rank-4 `L × E` access factorial —
distinguishes local, environmental, redundant, relational and null access **for a single declared target**. It does
**not** by itself establish that an effect belongs specifically to entity A rather than B; that requires Stage E's
member factorial. The full rung-6 claim needs both. The design for D already exists on paper and is the strongest
asset the program has. It is **eleven missions and five human reviews away**, and every attempt to shorten that
distance is the failure mode this architecture exists to prevent.

## Where the program stops

The chain terminates immediately, with no further families, on any of:

- **M4 finds no admissible span.** Dimensional analysis of the frozen equations yields no coordinate region in
  which the both-regime question is even well posed. The substrate is closed; go to consolidation.
- **M6 returns `EXCLUSION_CONFIRMED`.** Persistence and turnover are mutually exclusive at the declared scales.
  This is a *result*, not a setback: it explains the frozen Stage-B failure structurally and closes the
  lattice-bond substrate honestly. Go to consolidation. **Do not restructure turnover and retry** — that would be
  an attempt to defeat a finding rather than test one.
- **M7 fails to reproduce.** The window was a sampling artifact. Go to consolidation.
- **Any stage's attrition cannot be fully partitioned**, or any threshold is found to trace to a previous family's
  outcome, or any prohibited shortcut is used. `FEASIBILITY_INVALID` or the stage's equivalent, fail-closed.

## Consolidation is a real destination, not a consolation

Route F remains the named backup and becomes primary at every stop condition above. The material that is already
publishable without any further execution:

1. the **identifiability theorem** — ownership is not identifiable under a large, explicitly enumerated
   intervention algebra, with a constructive statement of what a sufficient algebra requires;
2. the **frozen `DEV_FEASIBILITY_FAIL`** as a clean prospective negative with one-shot execution discipline;
3. the **`AUDIT_INVALID` autopsy** as a worked example of a fail-closed analysis that refused to report partial
   products;
4. the **lattice-bond Phase-0 specification** with its rank-4 `L × E` design and its anti-manufacture table;
5. the **qualified lifecycle contract** as a reusable engineering pattern for prospective computational science.

That is a coherent methodological paper about what it takes to *ask* the individuality question rigorously. It does
not require a single further world.

## Explicitly outside this roadmap

- Any diagnosis of *why* the frozen Stage-B family failed. That requires a fresh, explicitly authorized, pre-data
  reconstruction in its own namespace, and it must not feed M4's span (see the report, §25 contamination barriers).
- **Moved into the chain as M0.** The empty-right-frame / non-unit-cadence repair is no longer optional: an entity
  that dissolves before the horizon produces an empty right detector frame at non-unit cadence, so under any
  hypothesis where dissolution is common a large fraction of worlds would be globally rejected rather than
  classified — biasing the primary estimand toward the surviving outcome class. See report §30.
- Any reopening, retry, subset selection or threshold revision of the closed Stage-B family.
- Reproduction, inheritance and genome work (frozen Stage F). It is beyond Stage D and beyond this roadmap.
- Anything in the Kovacs line, which remains independent and closed.
