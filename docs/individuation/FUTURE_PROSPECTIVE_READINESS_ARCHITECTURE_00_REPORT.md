### 2. Scientific target (frozen)

The target is **not** "does the system remember", "do entities survive", "do entities behave
differently", or "is there a lineage". Those are all already-answered or cheap questions.

The target is:

> **Can an intervention applied to one persistent organizational entity be shown to change that
> entity's own future behaviour, in a way that is not explained by its environment, by a redundant
> copy of its state, or by a relation it participates in — under material turnover, in a prospectively
> declared design, with no selection on outcome?**

Everything below exists to stop that sentence from being quietly weakened.

### 3. Claim ladder (frozen)

Every route must state the **highest rung it can identify** and must explicitly list the rungs it
leaves unsupported.

| Rung | Claim | Status in the frozen program |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A state variable stores past exposure | previously demonstrated at a low-dimensional level |
| 2 | That state causally affects later behaviour | previously demonstrated at a low-dimensional level |
| 3 | The state persists across material turnover | contested; the frozen Stage-B family failed on structural integrity before this could be answered |
| 4 | Two entities can be causally addressed separately | **open** |
| 5 | Intervention on A changes A's future differently from B's | **open** |
| 6 | The effect belongs specifically to A rather than its environment, a redundant copy, or a relation | **open — this is the rung that matters** |
| 7 | Evidence supports individual organizational ownership | **open; not reachable by any single family** |

**Vocabulary discipline.** "Survival", "memory", "lineage", "persistence" and "different responses"
are **not** synonyms for individuality and may not be substituted for rungs 5–7. A route that reaches
rung 3 and calls it individuality is inadmissible regardless of how clean its statistics are.

### 4. Frozen scientific baseline (preserved facts)

1. Causal experience memory has previously been demonstrated **only at a low-dimensional level**.
2. Memory being causal, erasable, transplantable or turnover-resistant is **not sufficient** for
   individuality.
3. The frozen Stage-B result is **`DEV_FEASIBILITY_FAIL`**.
4. That failure reflected **structural integrity loss** such as `SPLIT` and `LOST` — **not** proof that
   material turnover is impossible.
5. The failure **does not establish absence of individuality**.
6. The Stage-B family and its autopsy are **closed**.
7. Historical survivors **may not** be selected or retried.
8. **Stage C was never authorized.**
9. Local, environmental, redundant and relational ownership were **not identifiable** under the
   previous intervention algebra.
10. The **scaffold architecture STOP remains valid**.
11. The later lattice-bond substrate **routes around** the scaffold STOP but **does not silently prove
    ownership**.
12. The lifecycle gate is qualified **only in the new skeleton** and is **not installed in a real
    runner**.
13. The **empty-right-frame / non-unit-cadence path remains rejected**.
14. All Kovacs work remains **independent and closed**.

### 5. Mandatory admissibility gates (frozen — hard, pass/fail, applied before any comparison)

A route that fails **any** gate is inadmissible. Gates are not tradeable against strengths.

| Gate | Requirement |
|---|---|
| **G1 — Estimand declared** | The route states one exact estimand in advance, and states its unit: entity, lineage, pair or environment. |
| **G2 — No outcome selection** | No filtering to survivors, no replacement of failed units, no retry with nearby seeds, no post-hoc eligibility. |
| **G3 — No historical tuning** | No duration, threshold, cadence, dose or window derived from a previous family's outcomes. |
| **G4 — Competing-risk honesty** | Post-randomization failure is an **outcome or competing event**, never an exclusion. Any survival-conditioned quantity is declared as such and is not the primary estimand. |
| **G5 — Ownership confrontation** | The route explicitly confronts the environmental, redundant-copy and relational alternatives to entity-ownership. A relabelled version of the rejected environmental port is inadmissible. |
| **G6 — Turnover evidence** | If the route claims turnover, it states what **independent** evidence establishes that material turnover occurred, separately from the effect being measured. |
| **G7 — Lifecycle exhaustiveness** | Exactly one terminal lifecycle state per track; global rejection on any local violation; no successful subset may redefine the family. |
| **G8 — Stage separation** | Synthetic qualification, feasibility-only qualification, independent prerequisite reproduction and scientific execution are **separate families**. No family serves two roles unless a frozen, non-adaptive design justifies it. |
| **G9 — Interpretable failure** | A negative result must remain publishable and interpretable. If the route can only produce a paper when it succeeds, it is inadmissible. |
| **G10 — No engineered answer** | The route states how it avoids simply building individuality into the substrate, and what independent prerequisite reproduction is required before any individuality claim. |
| **G11 — Cadence honesty** | The route cannot silently enter the rejected empty-right-frame / non-unit-cadence path. |
| **G12 — Software is not evidence** | Lifecycle qualification, manifests, digests and viewers are engineering artifacts. None of them is scientific evidence for any rung. |

### 7. Prohibited shortcuts (frozen)

Any route relying on any of the following is rejected outright:

- filtering to historical survivors;
- replacing failed worlds;
- retrying with nearby seeds;
- tuning duration or thresholds on previous outcomes;
- weakening the tracker;
- ignoring split or merge events;
- declaring split descendants to be the same individual;
- conditioning the scientific outcome on post-treatment survival without a competing-risk estimand;
- adding memory channels, a genome or reproduction merely to make individuality easier;
- using a prettier viewer as evidence;
- calling scalar memory individuality;
- treating software qualification as scientific evidence;
- retrospective threshold revision;
- a permissive empty-right-frame cadence path;
- merging feasibility and scientific confirmation into one family.

### 9. Route-selection rule (frozen)

1. Apply the twelve gates. Every failure is recorded with the specific gate and the specific reason.
2. Among admissible routes, compare on the 22 dimensions **without weighting**.
3. Prefer the route with the **highest identifiable claim-ladder rung** that does **not** change the
   unit of analysis away from the entity. A route that changes the estimand from individual to lineage
   may still be recommended, but only if that change is stated **prominently** and it is **not**
   presented as solving the original question.
4. Where two routes reach the same rung, prefer the one whose **negative result is more informative**.
5. **Route F wins by default** unless some other route both (a) passes all gates and (b) can reach a
   rung strictly above 3 with an entity-level unit, or reaches rung 3 with a materially better
   identifiability argument than the frozen program already has.
6. If no route passes all gates, the disposition is `STOP_PROSPECTIVE_READINESS`.
7. At most one backup route may be named. A backup must independently pass all gates.
