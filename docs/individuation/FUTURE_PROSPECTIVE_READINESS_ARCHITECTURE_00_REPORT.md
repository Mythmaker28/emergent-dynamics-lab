### 2. Scientific target (frozen)

The target is **not** "does the system remember", "do entities survive", "do entities behave
differently", or "is there a lineage". Those are all already-answered or cheap questions.

The target is:

> **Can an intervention applied to one persistent organizational entity be shown to change that
> entity's own future behaviour, in a way that is not explained by its environment, by a redundant
> copy of its state, or by a relation it participates in — under material turnover, in a prospectively
> declared design, with no selection on outcome?**

Everything below exists to stop that sentence from being quietly weakened.

### 3. Claim ladder (frozen)

Every route must state the **highest rung it can identify** and must explicitly list the rungs it
leaves unsupported.

| Rung | Claim | Status in the frozen program |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | A state variable stores past exposure | previously demonstrated at a low-dimensional level |
| 2 | That state causally affects later behaviour | previously demonstrated at a low-dimensional level |
| 3 | The state persists across material turnover | contested; the frozen Stage-B family failed on structural integrity before this could be answered |
| 4 | Two entities can be causally addressed separately | **open** |
| 5 | Intervention on A changes A's future differently from B's | **open** |
| 6 | The effect belongs specifically to A rather than its environment, a redundant copy, or a relation | **open — this is the rung that matters** |
| 7 | Evidence supports individual organizational ownership | **open; not reachable by any single family** |

**Vocabulary discipline.** "Survival", "memory", "lineage", "persistence" and "different responses"
are **not** synonyms for individuality and may not be substituted for rungs 5–7. A route that reaches
rung 3 and calls it individuality is inadmissible regardless of how clean its statistics are.

### 14. What the frozen record actually says — two corrections that drive everything

**Correction 1 — the Stage-B failure was not primarily a `SPLIT`/`LOST` failure.**

The mission brief describes the frozen failure as "structural integrity loss such as `SPLIT` and `LOST`". The
frozen aggregate regime atlas does not support that as the *dominant* mode. Of the enrolled worlds, the largest
class by far was **persistence without turnover**, followed by static shell/crystal and dissolution; the
bounded-active-turnover class was rare and, critically, **`TRACKING_UNRESOLVED` did not occur at all**, and neither
did `TURNOVER_WITHOUT_PERSISTENCE`, `PERCOLATED` or `ACTIVE_UNBOUNDED`. Every shard completed, every step replayed
byte-identically, and no instrumentation or tracking-invalid status occurred.

The honest reading is that the substrate exhibits a **persistence/turnover trade-off**: at the declared horizon,
configurations that hold together tend not to exchange material, and configurations that exchange material tend to
dissolve. That is a **phase-structure** problem, not a tracking problem.

**No parameter, law index, region, horizon, lattice size, cadence or duration from that family is carried forward
anywhere in this document.** The observation is used only to *reject* routes premised on the wrong failure — never
to select a parameter. This is the single most contestable judgment in this package and it was handed to both
reviewers explicitly.

**Correction 2 — the failure mechanism is formally unknown, and knowably unknown.**

The Stage-B autopsy returned `AUDIT_INVALID`. It states that the complete world-level transition table, the
candidate-duration and censoring analysis, the formation-versus-maintenance decomposition and the parameter-region
descriptive structure are **"intentionally absent rather than partially reported"**, and that *"no conclusion is
permitted about brief threshold crossings, IC dependence, freezing, destructive-transition proximity, horizon
censoring, candidate coherence or absence of a coherent mechanism."*

So the program does not know *why* Stage B failed, and the only path to knowing is a fresh, explicitly authorized,
pre-data reconstruction in a new namespace — which this mission is not authorized to perform and which the current
human decision has not authorized.

**Consequence.** Any route whose design is motivated by a *specific* failure mechanism is building on evidence the
program does not have. This eliminates more routes than any other single fact in this analysis.

### 15. Where the ownership question actually stands

`OWNERSHIP_IDENTIFIABILITY_00` proves the requirement precisely. For the five competing models — local `H_L`,
environmental `H_E`, redundant `H_R`, relational `H_S`, narrow null `H_0` — identification of the saturated
availability decomposition `(β₀, β_L, β_E, β_LE)` requires a **rank-4 design with positive support on all four
cells plus consistency and exclusion**. The old substrate had **no admissible generator** for either
`do(a_L=0, a_E=1)` or `do(a_L=1, a_E=0)`; every available operation either destroyed the source state, acted
globally, replaced the boundary, or changed the estimand from access to formation.

Critically, the corollary states that even a clean member-level `K00/K10/K01/K11` factorial is **not sufficient** to
separate a pure environmental owner from `H_0`: a **second orthogonal access generator** is required.

**That generator already exists on paper.** `INTERVENTIONAL_INDIVIDUALITY_00` Phase 0 specifies, for the
lattice-bond substrate, a local port `q_L` gating a pre-existing `h → R_native` transduction edge and an
environmental port `q_E` gating **both orientations** of one declared pre-existing resource flux on physical
high-bond faces, with equal-and-opposite endpoint updates. The `L × E` design matrix has **algebraic rank 4**, and
the document is explicit that algebraic rank is not causal rank until every mechanical gate passes.

**This is the decisive structural fact of the whole program:** the hard part of rung 6 — the missing second access
generator — is *designed*. What is missing is not the ownership assay. What is missing is a substrate state in
which there is anything to address: an operational entity that both persists **and** turns over. That is Stage B,
and Stage B failed.

The staged program is `A → B → C → D → E → F`. It is blocked at **B**. `D` is the ownership factorial. Pulling `D`
forward is not merely unauthorized, it is meaningless: there is no persistent-through-turnover entity to cut.

### 16. Route analysis

#### Route A — turnover restructuring

**Design.** Shorten, stage, accelerate or event-trigger the turnover process while preserving the causal question.
Two sub-variants: **A1** clock-restructured (shorter or staged turnover windows) and **A2** event-triggered
(turnover driven by an intrinsic local event rather than a global clock).

**Gate result: A1 FAILS G3. A2 PASSES all gates, conditionally.**

- **A1 fails G3.** Choosing "shorter" or "staged" requires choosing *how much shorter*. The only information that
  could calibrate that is the previous family's outcome distribution. There is no first-principles length scale for
  "just enough turnover to be detectable but not enough to dissolve" that is independent of the observed phase
  boundary. A1 is retry-with-tuning wearing a new name. It also fails the prohibited-shortcut list explicitly
  ("tuning duration or thresholds on previous outcomes").
- **A2 survives.** Event-triggered turnover changes the *mechanism class* rather than a parameter: the trigger is
  defined by an intrinsic, dimensionally-derived condition on local state, not by a clock constant chosen to make
  entities survive. Changing mechanism class in response to a published negative feasibility result is ordinary
  science, not tuning — **provided** the trigger's functional form and any constants come from dimensional analysis
  of the frozen equations by an agent that has not read the regime atlas (see §21, contamination barriers).
- **G6 (turnover evidence).** Both variants must supply turnover evidence independent of the effect being measured.
  The frozen anti-manufacture table already licenses exactly one instrument for this: **passive cohorts** —
  material-origin diagnostics that may never enter flux, bond, detector, tracker assignment or port support. A2
  therefore carries a mandatory passive-cohort tracer whose *only* role is to establish that material actually
  cycled.

**Highest rung: 3.** Unit: entity. It establishes nothing above persistence-through-turnover.

**Assessment.** Admissible as A2 only. Its weakness is its negative result: a second feasibility failure of the
same shape would be only weakly informative, because without the diagnosis from Correction 2 the program still
would not know whether the window is narrow, absent, or mis-measured.

#### Route B — lineage-aware terminal semantics

**Design.** Treat `SPLIT`, `MERGE` and `LOST` as predeclared lifecycle information rather than retrospective
exclusions, and analyse at the lineage level.

**Gate result: FAILS G1 and G5 as an individuation route; PASSES as a lineage route with a reduced ceiling.**

- The estimand changes from **entity** to **lineage**. The frozen ontology is explicit that "fission is not
  automatically reproduction" and that a split event is *"initially a many-to-many tracker event"*. Declaring
  descendants to be the same individual is on the prohibited-shortcut list.
- Therefore Route B **cannot answer the original question**. It answers a different, legitimate question about
  lineage-level persistence of organization.
- **Route B is not primarily solving a measured problem.** Per Correction 2, the program does not know that
  `SPLIT`/`MERGE`/`LOST` were the binding constraint; the aggregate atlas suggests the binding constraint was
  persistence-without-turnover. Route B is a well-engineered answer to an unverified diagnosis.
- Its lifecycle *semantics* are nevertheless excellent and are **adopted wholesale into the architecture** (§17),
  independently of whether Route B is selected as the scientific route.

**Highest rung: 3, at lineage unit.** If presented as individuation, it would be a category error.

#### Route C — new substrate or intervention geometry

**Design.** Change structural geometry to improve integrity without tuning against historical outcomes.

**Gate result: FAILS G8 and G10 at this point in the program.**

- The lattice-bond substrate **is already** the Route-C answer. It was selected in `INTERVENTIONAL_INDIVIDUALITY_00`
  Phase 0 precisely because a lattice face is a fixed physical carrier for an antisymmetric flux, allowing a
  localized intervention that updates both endpoints equally and oppositely — the property the old scaffold
  substrate lacked. Changing substrate *again*, before the current one has been characterized, would discard the
  only asset the program has (a designed rank-4 access algebra) and restart at Stage A.
- **G10 risk is severe.** The frozen anti-manufacture table requires that at least one no-entity regime remain
  reachable under the same state schema, and that must-fail families (uniform/no-bond, open sheets, percolation,
  dissolution, impermeable shells, complete leakage) stay reachable. A geometry chosen *because it improves
  integrity* is a geometry chosen to make entities more likely — which is engineering the answer.
- The frozen revalidation burden makes the cost explicit: any new architecture requires the full ten-item chain and
  *"the first scientific claim in a modified substrate would be a new persistence/turnover result, not an ownership
  result."*

**Rejected** for this decision. It is the correct route only if the current substrate is characterized and closed.

#### Route D — addressability / intervention redesign

**Design.** Construct a new causal-intervention algebra or paired-counterfactual design separating local,
environmental, redundant and relational hypotheses.

**Gate result: PASSES G1, G4, G5 on paper; FAILS G8 (stage separation) as a *next* step.**

- Route D is **already designed and already correct**: the `L × E` rank-4 factorial with disjoint local
  transduction and boundary-resource ports, both compiler orders, matched shams, frozen `t0` supports, and a
  primary response `Q` that is a separately-frozen downstream native behaviour rather than the manipulated edge.
- It confronts the earlier rank deficiency explicitly and is **not** a relabelled environmental port: the frozen
  design forbids destination-only gating, state clamps, unlogged work, arbitrary detector cuts and using crossing
  flux itself as `Q`.
- It cannot be the next step. It is Stage D. It presupposes Stage C (an experience-dependent field in a requalified
  substrate), which presupposes Stage B (entities that persist through turnover), which failed.

**Highest rung: 6** — the only route in the set that can reach it. **It is the destination, not the next move.**
Recording it as the destination is the most important forward-looking statement this mission can make.

---

#### Route E — nonmodal: make the persistence/turnover trade-off the estimand

**Design.** Stop treating "bounded active turnover" as a nuisance precondition to be engineered into existence.
Make the **phase structure itself** the scientific object.

Prospectively test the structural hypothesis:

> **H_X (exclusion):** in this class of local-rule lattice-bond substrates, operational persistence and material
> turnover are mutually exclusive at the declared scales — every configuration that maintains a closed high-bond
> boundary over the declared horizon does so by suppressing material exchange, and every configuration with
> sustained exchange fails to maintain a boundary.

against

> **H_W (window):** there exists a non-empty region of the declared dimensionless coordinate space in which both
> hold simultaneously.

The declared coordinate space is a **dimensionless-group span derived from the frozen equations by dimensional
analysis**, in a code-only mission, by an agent that has **not** read the Stage-B regime atlas. The primary
estimand is the existence and measure of the both-regime set over that declared span, with the regime
classification frozen in advance and the passive-cohort turnover tracer supplying independent turnover evidence.

**Gate result: PASSES all twelve gates.**

- **G1** — one estimand, declared: measure of the both-regime set over a predeclared span; unit is the world.
- **G2/G3** — no selection, and the span comes from the equations, not from any previous outcome. The
  atlas-blindness requirement is a hard contamination barrier, not an aspiration.
- **G4** — dissolution, freezing, split, merge and loss are **outcomes**, not exclusions. They are the data.
- **G5** — not applicable at rung 3, and the route does not pretend otherwise.
- **G6** — passive cohorts give turnover evidence independent of the classifier.
- **G9 — this is where Route E is decisively stronger than Route A.** *Both* answers are publishable. If `H_X`
  holds, the program has a real structural finding about a substrate class, which simultaneously explains the
  frozen Stage-B failure without reopening it and honestly closes the individuation programme for this substrate.
  If `H_W` holds, the both-regime set was found **prospectively, without tuning**, and it is the legitimate,
  uncontaminated entry to Stage C.
- **G10** — the route cannot engineer the answer because the answer it is designed to detect *includes* "no
  entities anywhere in the span".

**Highest rung: 3.** Unit: world (for the phase claim) with entity-level regime classification.

**Assessment.** Route E subsumes Route A's success case — a Route-E family that finds a both-regime *is* Route A's
goal, reached without tuning — while having a strictly more informative negative. It also does **not** depend on
the missing Correction-2 diagnosis, because it presumes no mechanism.

#### Route F — stop and consolidate

**Design.** Halt experimental expansion. Consolidate the qualified negative and engineering results — the frozen
`DEV_FEASIBILITY_FAIL`, `AUDIT_INVALID`, `STOP-OWNERSHIP-IDENTIFIABILITY` with its rank/exclusion theorem, the
lattice-bond Phase-0 specification with its rank-4 `L × E` design, and the qualified lifecycle contract — into a
paper about what it takes to *ask* the individuality question rigorously.

**Gate result: PASSES all twelve gates trivially** — it makes no causal claim, runs nothing, and selects nothing.

**Highest rung: reports rungs 1–2 as previously established at a low-dimensional level; claims nothing above.**

**Assessment.** This is a genuinely strong comparator and it does not lose by default. The identifiability theorem
alone — that ownership is not identifiable under a large, explicitly enumerated intervention algebra, with a
constructive statement of what a sufficient algebra would require — is a publishable methodological contribution
independent of whether any entity is ever found. Route F's weakness is that it forecloses the one thing the
program has that is genuinely novel and designed: the rank-4 `L × E` access factorial.

### 19. Lifecycle semantics for the future family

The baseline rule under examination — pre-randomization failure counts against feasibility and cannot be replaced;
post-randomization failure remains an outcome or competing event and cannot be excluded; exactly one terminal state
per track; no successful subset may redefine the family — is **adopted**. Route E has no randomization, so
"pre/post-randomization" is read as **pre/post the declared observation window opening**.

| Case | Feasibility family (Route E) | Later scientific family (Stage C/D) |
|---|---|---|
| pre-window `SPLIT` | world is enrolled and classified; the pre-window split is a **formation outcome**, never a reason to re-draw | unit is undefined ⇒ the world is **ineligible**, counted in attrition, **not replaced** |
| pre-window `MERGE` | same: formation outcome | ineligible, counted, not replaced |
| pre-window `LOST` | same: formation outcome | ineligible, counted, not replaced |
| post-window `SPLIT` | **primary outcome data** — this is precisely what `H_X` vs `H_W` is about | **competing event**; the entity-level estimand is a cumulative-incidence quantity, never a survivor-conditioned mean |

**Is the rule sufficient?** For Route E, **yes** — because split/merge/loss are the outcome, so the rule's only

response among entities that remain identity-preserving, (ii) a lineage-level quantity, or (iii) a composite. Route
B's contribution is exactly this, and it is adopted as a *semantic* requirement even though Route B is not the
selected scientific route.

### 21. Provenance decision

**Content addressing alone is not sufficient for Route E.** The qualified lifecycle contract is explicitly
content-addressed and directory-portable, and its own qualification record states that the completion manifest
"carries no independent authority". That is adequate for an engineering gate and inadequate for a scientific claim,
because a scientific claim must be attributable to a specific declared procedure, not merely to some bytes.

**Required bindings for a future scientific package:**

| Binding | Required | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| exact Git commit | **yes** | the only stable name for the whole procedure |
| engine and tracker hashes | **yes** | edges from a different tracker are a different measurement |
| `LawSpec`, `WorldSpec`, `RunSpec` | **yes** | the declared design |
| RNG algorithm and initial state | **yes** | without it, "prospective" is unverifiable |
| sampling schedule | **yes** | already bound by the lifecycle contract |
| lifecycle evidence | **yes** | already qualified |
| association-edge evidence | **yes** | §20 |
| runner identity | **yes** | distinguishes the qualified path from an ad-hoc script |
| environment / dependency lock | **yes** | numerical reproducibility |
| preregistration identity | **yes** | this is what makes the claim prospective rather than exploratory |
| analysis-code identity | **yes** | the gate says nothing about what analysis is run |

**Reproducibility provenance versus cryptographic trust.** Everything above delivers *reproducibility* provenance:
given the artifacts, an independent party can regenerate byte-identical evidence and check that the declared
procedure was the one executed. **None of it delivers cryptographic trust.** A SHA-256 digest proves byte identity;
it does not prove who produced the bytes, when, or that they were not produced by a second run whose first result
was discarded. The only trust anchors in this program are the human-review records, and every one of them states
in its own text that no cryptographic signature is claimed. The architecture must **not** be described as
tamper-proof. The honest formulation is: *self-consistent, independently reproducible, and attributable only to the
extent that the human-review chain is trusted.*

The anti-discard property — that no earlier run was silently thrown away — is **not** obtainable from digests at
all. It is obtainable only from the frozen one-shot execution discipline the program already uses (exactly one
runner invocation, declared before data, no retry, no replacement) and from the human-review record that binds it.

**Requirement.** No future analysis entry point may accept an `AnalysisAccess` as a parameter, and none may use

The capability must be **non-transferable and non-persistent**: created inside the call, used immediately,

### 23. Empty-right-frame decision

**Decision: keep the path rejected. Require the future runner to provide complete frame pairs at the declared
cadence.** No tracker repair is undertaken now.

**Why Route E can operate without silently entering the rejected path.** Route E's sampling schedule is declared in

can proceed.

If a future route genuinely requires the rejected cadence path, that is a **separate tracker repair and

No family serves two roles.

**No-replacement rule.** Enrollment is fixed at declaration. A world that fails for any reason is recorded, counted
in attrition, and **never replaced**. The denominator is the enrolled set, always.

**Attrition accounting.** Every enrolled world resolves to exactly one terminal state. Attrition is reported as a
complete partition of the enrolled set, with pre-window and post-window failures separated. A family whose
attrition cannot be fully partitioned is `FEASIBILITY_INVALID`, not "mostly complete".

**Eligibility.** Declared before data, computed from pre-window state only. Post-window information may never enter
eligibility — that is the survivorship trapdoor, and it is closed by construction.

**Competing events.** Post-window `SPLIT`, `MERGE`, `LOST` and dissolution. In stage 2 they are the outcome; in
stage 4 they are competing risks with a predeclared cumulative-incidence estimand.

**Thresholds without historical tuning.** Every threshold comes from one of exactly three admissible sources:
(a) analytic or dimensional bounds derived from the frozen equations; (b) code-only synthetic qualification against
constructed must-pass and must-fail cases; (c) a declared invariant such as exhaustive single-terminal accounting.
**No threshold may be derived from any previous family's outcome.** The frozen Phase-0 contract already imposes
this on the detector; the architecture extends it to every threshold in the chain.

**When the program must stop.** Immediately, and without further families, if: the atlas-blind span declaration
finds no admissible span; stage 2 returns `EXCLUSION_CONFIRMED`; stage 3 fails to reproduce; any stage's attrition
cannot be partitioned; any historical outcome is found to have influenced any threshold; or any prohibited shortcut
is used.

### 30. Correction to the cadence decision (Part II §23 chose the wrong option)

The frozen limitation is a **conjunction**: an empty right detector frame **and** non-unit cadence, asserting both

are *sampled*; it cannot control whether the right detector frame is *empty*, which is determined by physics.

An entity that dissolves before the horizon produces an empty right detector frame at non-unit cadence. Under any
hypothesis in which dissolution is a common outcome — and dissolution was among the frequent classes in the frozen

(`detect → track → cohort → classify`) guarantees the empty frame reaches the tracker **before** classification
can record it as an outcome. Those worlds would be routed to global rejection rather than classified.

**The consequence is the exact failure this architecture exists to prevent:** systematic removal of the worlds
that support one hypothesis biases the primary estimand toward the other. The survivorship trapdoor is reopened
through the cadence door.

precede runner wiring.** Part II §23's option 1 — keep the path rejected and require complete frame pairs — is

**G11 was therefore not demonstrated for Route E**, and Part I's gates are hard and non-tradeable. Route E's claim
