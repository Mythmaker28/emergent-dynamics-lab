## 3. Cadence / survivorship blocker accepted

Reviewer B's finding is accepted in full.

The rejected tracker path is the **conjunction** of:

- an **empty right detector frame**; and
- **non-unit cadence**.

A declared sampling schedule does **not** prevent the physics from producing an empty right frame. Dissolution or
disappearance can naturally produce this condition. If the tracker/lifecycle stack **globally rejects** such runs
instead of classifying the terminal event, the recorded family becomes **outcome-dependent**: surviving entities
remain classifiable while dissolving entities may be rejected.

**That is an unacceptable survivorship mechanism.**

It is recorded that:

- the future schedule **does not discharge** the limitation;
- the earlier cadence decision is **reversed**;
- a separate **source-only tracker repair and requalification is mandatory** before any real-runner wiring;
- **no prospective feasibility or scientific family may open before this blocker is closed.**
