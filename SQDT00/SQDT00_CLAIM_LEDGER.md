# SQDT00_CLAIM_LEDGER

Append-only. What this programme claims, and — as importantly — what it refuses to claim.

## Claimed

1. **Provenance.** The parent chain resolves from the committed branch with 12/12 single
   direct-parent arrows; the `FWL2CF00` content is verified 198/198 twice, in two containers, with
   two git versions agreeing on the subtree id; the bundle verifies.

2. **Exact rederivation.** Rebuilding the reader series from the committed raw rho bytes on the
   union support reproduces the committed series **string-for-string** in exact rational form
   (48/48), which is simultaneously the support-restricted sufficiency certificate. The exact
   `R0` reproduces the parent's committed 1077-digit rational **bit-for-bit**. `R1, R2, I1, I2`
   are reproduced to certified rational enclosures of width ≈ 5·10⁻²³, and the parent's float
   values agree within the parent's own declared backward-stability bound.

3. **Serialized transferable basis.** `FWL2_RELATIVE_QUOTIENT_BASIS_V1` is fitted to the FWL2CF00
   fresh active panel, in the FWL2CF00 gauge and coordinates, and serialized as **real
   machine-readable arrays** (`mu, e1, e2, P1, P2`, per-row scores, sign canonicalisation, gauge
   signs). All eight stability gates `BASIS_S0…S7` pass, so `P2_TRANSFER_LICENSE` and the nested
   `E2_AXIS_TRANSFER_LICENSE` are **granted**. This removes precisely the defect that made the
   parent's stratum transfer `NOT_EVALUABLE_FROM_COMMITTED_PARENT_OBJECT`: a basis can be
   projected onto; a scalar cannot.

4. **Duplication invariance.** Exact row duplication leaves `R0`, `I2` and every ratio unchanged
   (proof + exact numeric), which licenses comparing an 8-descendant panel to the 32-row parent.

5. **Certified multipliers.** From the enclosures, never from the rounded 0.570:
   `ENERGY_MULTIPLIER_REQUIRED = 3.076` and `AMPLITUDE_MULTIPLIER_REQUIRED = 1.754`, both as
   certified intervals. The amplitude multiplier is **strictly below 2**.

6. **Static dose-axis inadmissibility.** The two locked carriers — `matched_transposition` and
   `intensive_reflection` — are involutions (`op² = identity`, bit-for-bit for carrier 1, to
   machine-ε for carrier 2), expose no scalar amplitude argument, already use a maximum-cardinality
   matching, and their γ=2 amplitude blend violates the frozen domain predicate. **No legitimate
   2× exists without a forbidden new executable.** The pre-execution oracle is non-vacuous across
   17 groups.

## Not claimed

* **Not** that the serialized basis is, recovers, or relates to any GIMB00 or founder-stratum
  object. It is not `GIMB00_STRATUM_AXIS`, `PARENT_FOUNDER_STRATUM`, `RECOVERED_PARENT_MODE` or
  `HISTORICAL_SECOND_MODE`. No such relation is established or implied.
* **Not** that the second quotient mode is physically real. It remains *relatively* present and
  *absolutely* under-powered, exactly as the parent found; SQDT00 adds a serialized handle for it,
  not a promotion of its status.
* **Not** that a dose response exists or was measured. `Q3/Q5` are `NOT_EVALUABLE`; `Q4` is
  `STATICALLY_INADMISSIBLE`. Zero fresh rows were created.
* **Not** that `AMPLITUDE_MULTIPLIER_REQUIRED < 2` means the mission succeeded. It means only that
  *if* a legitimate 2× existed and the response were linear, it *would* suffice. Existence fails;
  sufficiency is moot.
* **Not** any statement about geometry or allocation. Both are `NOT_TESTED_IN_THIS_DESIGN`.
* **Not** a distributional inference from `n = 8`. The panel was in any case never built.
