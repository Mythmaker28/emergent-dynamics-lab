## The question

Is the scientifically relevant individual (A) the locked spatial daughter, (B) the ambient
population, or (C) a distributed causal lineage rooted in the daughter? Answered by audit over the
66 existing OMLDCT02 paired archives. No world was run, no seed was drawn, no parameter was searched.

## What Model C turned out to be

It is reconstructible everywhere: all 66 arms, zero physical-invariant violations, built from cell
coordinates, occupancy and the three event ledgers alone. It reads no online identity, no verdict and
no future outcome.

It is **sound** — a property the checker established and I had not. Because the one-step transport
kernel is exactly the toroidal Moore neighbourhood, membership in CERTAIN implies that all of a
cell's Y mass descends from the daughter. The rule can under-claim; it can never over-claim.

It is **distinct from the locked component** in 62 of 66 arms, by a median duration factor of about
23 and a maximum of 10,667. It crosses spatial separation and constituent replacement without losing
the causal path — 1,404 replacements across 22 arms and 37,944 split rows across 23 arms in the
non-degenerate arms alone — and every one of those continuations carries an enumerable witness.

And in the **control** arm it is specific: where a competing ambient population exists, the median
share of post-A world mass it claims is 0.0074.

## Why it still fails

In the **treated** arm it is the ambient population. Not close to it — identical to it: CERTAIN
equals the full occupied set on **100 % of post-fork rows**, and its duration equals the ambient
duration exactly in 33 of 33. The only difference between C and B there is the single
pre-intervention row, where the archive still counts the parent about to be removed.

That is not a defect in the model. It is the intervention. Removing the parent leaves the daughter as
the world's only Y source, so "everything afterwards descends from the daughter" becomes true by
construction — and explains nothing. The object carries information in the arm that is the control
and none in the arm the experiment is about.
