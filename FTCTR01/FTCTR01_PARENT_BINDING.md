exists to *derive* the clock, not to retrieve it, and it would have proceeded happily from an
unnamed engine with no seals at all.

What stops it is that the **critical executable objects** are absent too — the discrete Y diffusion
implementation, the four ordered sub-shifts, the hop-probability path, the X decay path, the Y
birth/death paths, the candidate-pool semantics, the capacity-blocking semantics, the centre

not vocabulary: see `FTCTR01_EXECUTABLE_BYTE_AUDIT.md` Part 2, where four independent fingerprints of
a discrete stochastic hopping model are each shown to be absent (zero binomial or equivalent draws in
the entire history; no integer per-cell occupancy of a mobile species; every engine RNG confined to
