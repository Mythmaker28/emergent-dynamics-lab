# FSQBT00_INDEPENDENT_UNIT_REPORT

## Independent unit
The independent unit is the **fresh upstream ancestry block** — a distinct seed's
`found → advance → history → settle` precursor. There are exactly **twelve** such blocks (seeds
65100–65111), one descendant each. The two carriers, two channels, ten scored times, and the
support sites within a block are **repeated conditions, never replications**.

**n = 12.** It is never reported as 24 (rows), 48, the number of sites, or the number of times.

## Randomization / balance
The twelve blocks are drawn from the deterministic clean namespace N = 65100 by a metadata-only
scan, mapped into four `(geometry, allocation)` subqueues, round-robin, three acceptances per cell:

    NEAR_a0 : 65100, 65104, 65108
    NEAR_a1 : 65101, 65105, 65109
    FAR_a0  : 65102, 65106, 65110
    FAR_a1  : 65103, 65107, 65111

Twelve accepted in twelve construction starts (zero rejects). The nuisance labels
`(geometry, allocation)` are **balanced only**; they identify no geometry, allocation or interaction
effect, and none is claimed. `GEOMETRY_STATUS = ALLOCATION_STATUS = DOSE_STATUS =
NOT_TESTED_IN_THIS_DESIGN`.

## What the design can and cannot support
* It CAN test whether the frozen response-informed object transfers, without refit, to twelve fresh
  independent ancestry blocks under the two historical carriers at 1×.
* It CANNOT support a population inference, an individuality claim, or a geometry/allocation causal
  claim. A descriptive pattern in which the three tube-breaching blocks happen to be NEAR is recorded
  as hypothesis-generating only.

The stability of the transfer conclusion is assessed by leave-one-fresh-ancestry-block-out (twelve
folds) inside the transfer gates, exactly to keep the unit correct — the same lesson that the
`LOBO_VS_LODO_AUDIT` applied to the parent object.
