\section{Decision rule, uncertainty and diagnostics}
For an absolute endpoint, let $\bar r$ be the mean of the 14 arm medians and $p$ its
frozen prediction. The criterion of record is
\[
 |e|\le2.9,\qquad e=100(\bar r/p-1).
\]
Its sampling standard error is $100s/(\sqrt{14}p)$, with $s$ the sample standard deviation
across arms: \VStaticSE\% static and \VMobileSE\% mobile.
The predictor dispersions from stored SEAL01 replica means are
\VStaticMCSD\% and \VMobileMCSD\% on the seal's reported scale.
They describe repeated 30-arm predictor calculations, not extra engine arms.

For $R=\bar r_M/\bar r_S$, disjoint seed sets give the first-order relative standard error
\[
 s_{\rm rel}=\sqrt{(s_M/(\sqrt{14}\bar r_M))^2+
                       (s_S/(\sqrt{14}\bar r_S))^2}.
\]
On the scale $100(R/p_R-1)$ it becomes $100(R/p_R)s_{\rm rel}$, or \VRatioSE\%.
The historical adjudication omitted $R/p_R$ in this diagnostic scale conversion.
V2 records this small correction; the frozen point result is unchanged.
The reported ratio predictor dispersion \VRatioMCSD\% uses the inherited quadrature
propagation, not an independently simulated ratio distribution.

A whole-interval rule was not frozen. The post-freeze absolute equivalence diagnostics
use the smaller distance to the boundary and Student's $t$ upper tail with 13 degrees of freedom:
\[
 p_{\rm diagnostic}=
 {\Pr}\left(T_{13}\ge\frac{2.9-|e|}{\mathrm{SE}(e)}\right).
\]
They give \VStaticTOST{} static and \VMobileTOST{} mobile.
These probabilities condition on the point predictions; they do not integrate predictor
uncertainty or correct for model selection, and are not independent confirmatory evidence.
The original normal-tail probabilities are superseded.

\section{Reproduction and limits}
From the paper directory, run \path{python code/reproduce.py}.
This verifies the pinned source manifest, recomputes raw summaries, emits numerical macros,
tables and four figures, and checks claim bindings.
With Tectonic available, \path{python code/reproduce.py --tectonic /path/to/tectonic}
also rebuilds both PDFs. Python requirements and exact audited versions are supplied.

The source manifest is an input; the audit never refreshes it to make failing hashes pass.
The package includes existing raw data used by the figures.
PQEC01's larger raw package remains external. Its 128-file hash and schema audit is included
and can be repeated using \path{audit_final.py --pqec-raw} with the extracted raw directory.
Without that directory, a new build does not claim to have reverified those external bytes.

The final-lattice radius and digest are checked for every fresh arm.
Intermediate radii are checked against stored summaries because full intermediate
lattices are not archived here.
No independent engine replication, parameter sweep, manuscript peer review,
publication, DOI or merge is claimed.
