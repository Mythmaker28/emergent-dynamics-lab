\section{Provenance, freeze and inertness}

\subsection{What was frozen, and when}

Two absolute predictions and their ratio were written into a protocol file and hashed before any
confirmation arm existed. The frozen predictions carry the status \texttt{\predictionStatus{}}
and the methods hash carries \texttt{\methodsCoreHashStatus{}}. The methods hash itself is
\texttt{\methodsCoreHash{}}, taken over the files listed in Table~\ref{tab:methods}; the list of
files recorded as missing from that hash was empty.

\subsection{The apparatus does not perturb the system}

An instrumented engine and a plain one were run from the same seed for \inertnessSteps{} steps and
their full lattice states compared byte for byte. They are identical, with common final state hash

\smallskip
\noindent\hspace*{1em}\texttt{\inertnessStateHash{}}
\smallskip

\noindent
so that
\texttt{STATE\_\allowbreak{}IDENTICAL} \texttt{=} \texttt{\inertnessStateIdentical} and
\texttt{INSTRUMENTATION\_\allowbreak{}ALTERS\_\allowbreak{}THE\_\allowbreak{}LAW} \texttt{=}
\texttt{\inertnessAltersTheLaw}. This is a single paired run at a single seed. It is sufficient to
falsify interference and insufficient to prove inertness for every trajectory, and we claim only
the former.

\subsection{Evidence tiers}

Each programme contributing to this work carries one of five tiers, assigned mechanically from
what survives in the session that produced this paper rather than from what was once true:
\texttt{QUALIFIED} (recomputable from surviving hashed bytes and adjudicated in its own
programme), \texttt{DEVELOPMENTAL} (recorded, but not produced under a freeze that would let it
carry a claim), \texttt{INVALID} (adjudicated technically invalid), \texttt{LOST\_\allowbreak{}%
DOCUMENTARY} (the programme ran, its narrative record exists elsewhere, its bytes do not), and
\texttt{NOT\_\allowbreak{}TESTED}. The assignment is in
\texttt{provenance/\allowbreak{}PAPER\_\allowbreak{}SOURCE\_\allowbreak{}BINDING.json}. Every
confirmatory statement in the main text rests on \texttt{QUALIFIED} evidence only.

\section{The operator and the observable}

\subsection{Exact definition of the reported radius}

Let $w_i$ be the occupancy of cell $i$ and $d_i$ its toroidal distance to the source cell. Sort
the cells by $d$, form the cumulative sum of $w$ normalised by the total, and take
\[
r_q \;=\; d\!\left[\ \operatorname{searchsorted}\!\left(\ \frac{\operatorname{cumsum}(w_{\text{sorted by }d})}{N},\ q,\ \text{side}=\text{left}\right)\right],
\quad\quad q = 0.8 .
\]
Three properties of this estimator matter for Section~5 of the main text and are stated in the
source programme's own definition block: it is a finite-sample quantile of about $120$ particles
and not a population quantity; it is a \emph{first crossing}, that is a minimum over $d$, and
minima of noisy crossings are biased downwards; and it is supported on the discrete set of
achievable toroidal distances.

\subsection{Event order}

The within-step order was read from the kinetics module: transport of each species in turn, then
reaction, then decay, then feed and outflow. Because reaction runs after transport, a particle
born in step $t$ takes no step in step $t$; and because every one of the
\birthPositionTotalBirthRecords{} recorded births across \birthPositionArms{} arms occurred at the
source cell exactly, with maximum offsets \birthPositionMaxAbsDy{} and
\birthPositionMaxAbsDx{}, the point-source idealisation is a checked property rather than an
assumption.

\subsection{Where the closure fails}

Conditional on the state the operator is exact. Taking expectations fails because the transport
factor and the occupancy are functions of the same state. The relevant measured couplings, over
\couplingArms{} arms, are: births equalled the minimum of available substrate and free capacity in
\couplingFractionOfStepsWhereBirthsEqualMinnsxfree{} of steps, and the source cell had no free
capacity in \couplingFractionOfStepsWithZeroFreeCapacityAtTheOrganiser{} of them. Hence
\texttt{\closureFullOneStepConditionalOperator} for the conditional operator,
\texttt{\closureStationaryProfileClosure} for the stationary profile, and
\texttt{\closureMarginalDensityClosure} for the marginal density.

\section{The historical record and the estimator surrogate}

\subsection{The radial profile at every radius}

\input{S6_radial}

\noindent
The mobile column rests on \radialProfileMobileArms{} arms and its largest standardised deviation
is \radialProfileMaxAbsZ{}. The static column rests on \radialProfileStaticArms{} arms; its
largest standardised deviation is \radialProfileStaticMaxAbsZ{}, at a radius where the absolute
difference \radialProfileStaticMaxAbsDifference{} is no larger than at radii whose standardised
deviation is below one. A standard error estimated from three arms can collapse by chance, and
here it did. The flag recorded by the source programme is scoped
\texttt{\radialProfileFlagScope{}}, and we report the static cell as not evaluated. It is printed
above so that a reader can see exactly what was set aside, not because it supports anything.

\subsection{The surrogate}

The surrogate draws particles independently from the operator's own stationary law at the observed
per-frame counts and applies the identical summary rule, over \iidStaticArms{} surrogate arms of
\iidStaticFrames{} frames. It contains no lattice, no transport, no capacity and no source. Its
biases are $\cellStaticMedianSurrogatePercent\pc$ and $\cellStaticMeanSurrogatePercent\pc$ in the
static condition and $\cellMobileMedianSurrogatePercent\pc$ and
$\cellMobileMeanSurrogatePercent\pc$ in the mobile one. Note that in the static condition the mean
rule is the \emph{worse} of the two under the surrogate: the mean rule is not unbiased, only less
biased where it matters.

\section{Constructions, ablations and the terms that were dropped}

\input{S7_models}

\noindent
Removing the shared source trajectory collapses the within-arm standard deviation from
\mSixDispersionMobileWithinArmSdSimulated{} to \ablationNoSharedTrajectoryWithinArmSd{}, onto the
static width, and the median penalty collapses with it. Flattening the birth flux to a Poisson
source of the same mean costs \ablationPoissonBirthsLosesPercentagePoints{} percentage points.
In the two-by-two design the main effects are
\mSixFactorialMainEffectSharedTrajectory{} and \mSixFactorialMainEffectBirthFlux{} percentage
points with an interaction of $+\mSixFactorialInteraction{}$; the three quantities sum to the
total by construction, so their sum is not independent evidence for any of them.

\noindent
Capacity is negligible here for a reason that can be stated in one line: over a mean lifetime a
particle is offered \capacityPerLifetimeOfferedHops{} hops and expects
\capacityPerLifetimeExpectedRefusals{} refusals, so
\capacityCertifiedFractionNeverRefused{} of particles are never refused at all, and removing that
fraction of moves while concentrating every removal adversarially on outward moves changes the
reported radius by \capacityImpliedChangeInREightZeroPercent\pc{}. The birth flux, by contrast, is
over-dispersed at a variance-to-mean ratio of \birthFluxVarianceOverMean{} against $1$ for a
Poisson source, and it is retained.

\section{Reproducing the numbers in this paper}

Each of the \nReconciliationRows{} rows of
\texttt{provenance/\allowbreak{}PAPER\_\allowbreak{}NUMERICAL\_\allowbreak{}RECONCILIATION.json}
carries a manuscript label, a value, its units, its estimand, its unit of analysis, its evidence
tier, the source file and that file's SHA-256, the JSON path within the file, the recomputed
value, the rounding rule, and the sections in which the value may appear. The macros used by the
main text and this supplement are generated from that file by
\texttt{code/\allowbreak{}emit\_\allowbreak{}numbers.py}, so a number cannot appear in the text
unless it appears in the reconciliation first. The four main figures are regenerated by
\texttt{code/\allowbreak{}make\_\allowbreak{}figures.py}, and each writes a source-data file
alongside its PDF.
