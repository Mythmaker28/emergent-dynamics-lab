These seven form a short core. Statistical references may be added separately. Avoid filling the paper with every related condensate or morphogenesis review.

Final manuscript statistical addition, independently verified: [Bengio & Grandvalet (2004), No Unbiased Estimator of the Variance of K-Fold Cross-Validation](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v5/grandvalet04a.html), Journal of Machine Learning Research 5:1089–1105. The official publisher page verifies metadata and the covariance/variance concern. It does not supply a DOI; the publisher URL is used. It supports a general uncertainty limitation, not a known magnitude or direction of miscoverage in this dataset.

Optional comparisons: [Donau et al. (2020)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-18815-9) for fueled coacervates/component exchange; [Xiong & Ferrell (2003)](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature02089) for feedback intervention, citing or documenting its [2007 equation correction](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature06125) if its equations are discussed. Do not use an uncorrected equation as a benchmark.

The CCRA supplement can cite [Lo, Wilke & Emura (2024)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0167947324000379) for the distinction between observed cause-specific hazards and latent marginal hazards. The original CCRA rationale confuses them. Its ordinal test remains an explicitly chosen reanalysis; this reference does not validate that ranking.

Recent awareness item: [Gajendragad & Saha, arXiv v2 (March 2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17444v2), on history-dependent active-particle interactions. The inspected record has no journal reference; retain preprint status. It does not test local ownership or the EDL turnover endpoint.

Excluded as primary evidence: the Maity/Moschou condensate-memory article, DOI 10.1016/j.bpj.2025.11.2681, describes itself as a review/proposed framework. It may motivate experiments but cannot establish an experimentally verified mechanism for this paper.
