# Independent scientific review — complex systems/artificial life

Reviewer scope: model/construct validity, mechanistic discrimination and novelty. Candidate B and candidate C were read without a prescribed preferred conclusion. Historical reviewers and authors' verdicts were used as locators, not authority. Source-level checks and numeric material accounting appear in SOURCE_CHECK.json and MODEL_SOURCE_NOTE.md. The new manuscript is not yet reviewed; none of the following issues is declared resolved merely because the integrator accepted it.

## Recommendation

The strongest coherent manuscript is a bounded B study: an engineered history-dependent field remains predictive of assigned nutrient history and causally affects nutrient uptake after a prespecified decline in own-snapshot material fraction, while the prespecified predictive-superiority conjunction is unmet. This is an assay/benchmark result. Its scientific value lies in reporting positive content, causal expression and inconclusive access-scope comparisons together, with explicit algebraic baselines. It does not establish a new physical principle of memory survival and does not disprove local ownership or individuality.

C addresses a different problem: how a relational tracker responds when treatment changes neighbouring components and terminal-event composition. It is useful as a separate methodological example or supplement. Its composite does not add a more discriminating positive mechanism to B. FDFLT01's connected-centre/function criterion concerns yet another architecture and cannot supply missing ownership evidence. No pooling of these experiments is justified.

## Mechanistic and conceptual assessment

The own-erasure, neighbour-erasure, sham, fixed-mask and channel-ablation battery meaningfully tests whether an intervention on the represented field changes the chosen output within admissible tracked worlds. It does not identify which physical variable uniquely stores all historical information: history also perturbs density and other fields, and both history writing and readout are designed. The result is stronger than a static visual pattern but should not be sold as unexpected causal functionality.

Predictive superiority of a finite ridge representation is a reasonable deliberately stringent operational gate. It is neither a necessary definition of an individual nor an estimator of unique information. Different model capacities, coarse summaries, regularization and sample size enter its result. Even a positive gate would not prove literal exclusivity of all information; failure especially cannot prove absence. Information may be redundant with body and environment without being unusable or biologically unowned. Krakauer et al. explicitly distinguish several information-theoretic partitions, including shared and synergistic contributions; the present study does not compute those quantities.

For C, the classifier genuinely has relational semantics. Losing a merge opportunity under parent removal changes the dynamics and the event composition, so a scalar interval cannot distinguish independent self-maintenance from relief of a collision channel. That is a defensible interpretation limit. It is not a theorem that treatment must preserve every cause-specific risk to estimate any total effect. Relabelling the same no-link predicate can produce category-count artefacts, but combining distinct physical mechanisms into one rank sacrifices detail and does not remove mediation through the treatment-modified neighbourhood.

## Literature positioning

Primary-source verification is documented in VERIFIED_REFERENCES.json/.md. Particularly relevant antecedents are Lisman's model of memory despite molecular replacement (1985), engineered bistable memory (Gardner et al., 2000; Xiong and Ferrell, 2003, with 2007 equation correction), information-theoretic individuation (Krakauer et al., 2020), and intervention-based semantic information tied to a viability criterion (Kolchinsky and Wolpert, 2018). The present data do not test the latter's viability objective.

Recent ALife work raises the bar for broad claims: Flow-Lenia studies explicitly localize rule parameters and quantify evolutionary dynamics (Plantec et al., 2025), while Hintze and Bohm (2026) trace distributed causal ancestry and replication across non-contiguous formations. These are adjacent systems, not direct replications or controls. They underscore why one connected component or one high-performing feature vector cannot be assumed to define an individual. A 2025/2026 active-matter preprint also studies history-dependent interactions and pattern formation, but not the local-ownership gate; cite only if useful, not to inflate novelty.

The plausible original contribution is the released, prospectively fixed, original-world analysis of **three evidential questions on the same admissible simulated worlds**, accompanied by honest gate failure and reproducible negative controls. No exhaustive priority claim is supported by this targeted search. A specialized paper is defensible after correction; a field-changing flagship claim is not yet established.

## What would strengthen or falsify this assessment

The copy identity and exact update equations strengthen exposition without new simulations. Existing raw records can supply conditionality, distributions, leave-one-world sensitivity and decoder-baseline diagnostics. New representations should be explicitly exploratory and not overwrite the frozen decision. A future causal transfer or targeted error-correction assay could distinguish mechanisms, but would require a separately specified experiment and cannot be inferred from the present retained effect.

The bounded conclusion would fail if a reproducible data calculation removes the own-history or own-erasure effect, if persisted records do not correspond to the sealed code, or if material/tracking accounting invalidates the selected worlds. Those are for the statistical and clean-reproduction reviews as well as this manuscript review. Perfect arithmetic reproduction alone would not close construct-validity limitations.
