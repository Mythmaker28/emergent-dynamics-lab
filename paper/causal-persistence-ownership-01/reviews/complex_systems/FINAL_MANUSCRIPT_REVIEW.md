## Remaining text findings

**FR01 — MINOR, required for scientific precision: abstract tracer denominator.** At main-text line 9, “retained at most one quarter of the material marked in its own initial mask” can mean remaining labelled mass divided by the amount originally marked. That is not the implemented ratio. Replace it with, for example: “At most one quarter of each target's current mass carried its own snapshot label.” Methods already define the correct current-mass denominator. This is especially worth correcting because the abstract is often read alone.

**FR02 — MINOR, required for the exact-scheme claim: concentration floors and zeroing before internal diffusion.** Supplement S2 currently gives the correct transport/reaction/copying structure but does not explicitly define donor concentrations as extensive field divided by `max(rho, EPS)`. After transport/growth/removal, the engine reconstructs `u` and `v` with that denominator and sets them to zero on non-alive cells **before** evaluating the simultaneous reactions and `lap(u)`/`lap(v)`. Simply applying a final alive mask leaves a different boundary contribution to adjacent live cells if nonzero inactive concentrations were used inside the Laplacian. Add this ordering; retain the existing statement that the protected source is definitive. Memory reconstruction is also `Mf/max(rho,EPS)` but is not pre-zeroed in the same way before its update. The equations should preserve that distinction.

**FR03 — MINOR, required observation-definition detail: when tracked continuity begins.** At the end of the history and settling block, `cc.region_masks` chooses the nearest detected component to each original drive centroid. `turnover_engine_03g._storage` does not maintain a bijective identity track throughout the preceding two drive phases and settling. The bijective continuity test begins at material marking. Add this in S3.1 and, briefly, Methods 2.2. This does not refute the bounded claim that the selected snapshot representation predicts an assigned drive label; it prevents readers from assuming proven identity continuity through history writing.

**Optional wording precision:** Discussion's “Physical active droplets [5]” could read “physical models of active droplets [5]”. The verified Zwicker et al. article is a theoretical physical model; it should not inadvertently be presented as an experimental reproduction of the EDL result.
