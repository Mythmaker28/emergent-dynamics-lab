# LDFMA01 — SINGLE-SUCCESS AUTOPSY

Comparison families were fixed in `LDFMA01_SUCCESS_COMPARISON_PREREGISTRATION.json`, sha256
`ae708ba863c764dc553a953625e97ac8d63fcc4dab0a99ed6372e17d7b665db2`, written before the 21 failure
worlds were reconstructed.

**An honest limit, stated rather than glossed.** The identity of the sole success —
`TLMR01_LAW_C_MCTT01_P_i001_s1474284807` — was established by FIMRCC01 before this mission began,
and its own feature values were seen while the reconstruction was being smoke-tested on the only
archive staged at the time. The pre-registration cannot make the success label blind. What it does
buy is that the **feature set and the comparison rule were fixed before any failure distribution
existed**, which is what stops a feature being chosen after seeing which one separates.

**No significance test was run.** One success is not an independent population sample. No p-value,
no confidence interval, no effect size appears anywhere in this autopsy.

## What is actually unique about the success

Four of 30 comparable features lie outside the closed range of the 21 failures. Three of them are
restatements of the outcome itself:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| E | `n_Y_removals_after_t_m` | 1 | [0, 0] | no |
| E | `x_before_first_removal` | 81 | [0, 0] | no |
| E | `x_after_first_removal` | 81 | [0, 0] | no |
| **A** | **`f5_ratio_at_trigger`** | **1.0** | **[0.643, 0.989]** | **yes** |

The last three exist *only because* a removal was attributed. They are the endpoint, not an
explanation of it.

**Exactly one available-before-outcome feature is unique**: the trigger-time local-X ratio was
exactly 1.0, meaning the two centres had identical local X disc mass. With the whole world holding
**2 Y particles** at that trigger, two identical tiny discs is an unremarkable coincidence, and one
observation cannot distinguish a coincidence from a condition. It is reported, not built on.

## Seven candidate mechanisms the success falsifies

The success value sits **inside the interquartile span of the failures** for seven mechanistically
interpretable features, including the daughter's post-removal identity lifetime, its maximum
occupancy, its number of Y births, its local X mass at the trigger, the parent–daughter distance,
and the ambient pressure it faced. Any mechanism asserting that one of these must be extreme for a
locked daughter to complete a turnover is **falsified**.

## The one thing that separates the success

The success is the only world in which a **Y removal was attributed to the locked daughter under
the frozen rule**. Everything else about it is ordinary.

## What this means for a prospective design

**No available-before-outcome feature separates the success from the failures.** There is no
prospective eligibility criterion that could enrich a future experiment for locked-daughter
turnover — a design hoping to select promising worlds in advance has nothing to select on.
