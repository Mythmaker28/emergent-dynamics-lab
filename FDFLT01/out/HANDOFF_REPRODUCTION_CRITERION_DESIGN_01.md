## Why this successor exists

FDFLT01 shows that a second organising centre forms, matures its own X response and holds,
at a rate far above a 10 % rare-event null. That is a RECURRING FUNCTIONAL EVENT. It is not
yet reproduction, and the honest next move is not another experiment — it is to define what
reproduction would have to mean here, operationally, before anything is run to test it.

## The question this zero-run successor must answer

What additional operational conditions distinguish a recurring functional two-centre
lineage event from reproduction?

## What it must examine, at minimum

    material turnover
        Does the second centre's X cloud consist of material it acquired itself, or is it
        a redistribution of the first centre's? FDFLT01 measured the RATIO of local X mass
        (median 0.8226 at the maturation event). Ratio is not provenance.
        The src ledger and xevent records already recorded per step may settle this without
        any new run.

    parent/daughter functional independence
        If the first centre's source were removed, would the second persist? OBTC02 already
        contains a source-removal operator; ask whether an INDEPENDENCE criterion can be
        defined from the existing record before proposing an intervention.

    persistence after separation
        FDFLT01 observed two-centre episodes with a median maximum duration of 1872 steps
        and a maximum of 7308 steps, against a maturation time of 250. Persistence is
        abundant. Name the duration a reproduction claim would require and why.

    can each centre independently seed or maintain its own X response
        This is the operational core. Define it as a measurable predicate, not a narrative.

    can a daughter centre itself generate a further functional centre
        25 of 192 worlds reached three or more centres, always AFTER functional
        maturation, never before (0 of 192). Those 25 worlds are the natural place to
        ask whether a second generation is already visible in data that exists.
        NOTE the censoring: the frozen stop rule halts a world at the third centre, so what
        follows a third centre is unobserved. Say so rather than inferring past it.

    what constitutes one generation
    what minimal inheritance observable would be required
