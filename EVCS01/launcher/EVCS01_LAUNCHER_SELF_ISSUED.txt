======================================================================
1. WHY THIS MISSION AND NOT ANOTHER
======================================================================

CLEA01 closed with a finding aimed at the parent, not at itself:

  in at least 2 of 66 arms the qualified OMLDCT02 E3 component provably contains Y mass with no
  causal path to the daughter.

"At least 2" is a floor, not a measurement: the G4 containment test stopped at the first three
disagreeing rows per arm. The real quantity — in what fraction of arms, and how much of E3_EXPOSURE
— was never measured.

This matters because E3_DURATION and E3_EXPOSURE are not OMLDCT02's endpoints alone. They are the
programme's measuring instrument, and any successor campaign will reuse them. Auditing what an
instrument measures, before reusing it, is not a twenty-first angle on the lineage question. It is
the thing that should have preceded the lineage question.

Second, the pre-mission accrual note of 2026-08-26 established that two independent campaigns were
frozen with single-digit power:

  FIMRCC01   P(k>=2 | n=50, observed rate 1/256)      = 0.0165
  OMLDCT02   P(41 pairs | ceiling 512, realised rate) = 0.076

and that any future campaign sized from the TLMR01 developmental worlds will under-accrue by a
factor of about two. That note is not committed and not tested. A finding that lives only in a
delivered markdown file is the OMLDCT01 failure mode again. It is turned into committed, tested
code here.

3. GATE 0 — THE RECONSTRUCTION MUST BE THE OBJECT OMLDCT02 QUALIFIED
======================================================================

Before any composition is measured, reconstruct Model A per row from LDFMA01's frozen classifier and
recompute, for all 66 arms, the parent's own two numbers under the parent's own frozen conventions:

    E3_DURATION  = interval_end - t_m
    E3_EXPOSURE  = sum of nY over rows t_m .. interval_end     (t_m INCLUDED)

Compare against OMLDCT02_PAIR_MEASUREMENTS.json, classifier A, exactly.

    ALL 66 ARMS REPRODUCE  ->  proceed
    ANY ARM DISAGREES      ->  EVCS01_TECHNICALLY_INVALID, and the disagreement is published

There is no partial credit and no tolerance. If the object being decomposed is not bit-identical in
its aggregates to the object the parent qualified, the decomposition describes something else.

======================================================================
4. THE MEASUREMENT — THRESHOLD-FREE COMPOSITION OF E3_EXPOSURE
======================================================================

For every one of the 66 arms, over rows t_m .. interval_end, partition the Y mass inside Model A's
component into exactly three classes, using Model C's CERTAIN and POSSIBLE sets as already frozen
and committed in CLEA01 (imported unchanged, not re-specified):

    CERTAIN            all of this cell's Y mass provably descends from the locked daughter
    POSSIBLE_ONLY      a causal path exists but is not exclusive
    NO_CAUSAL_PATH     no admissible source of this cell is even POSSIBLE

Report, per arm and in aggregate, and as a DISTRIBUTION rather than a headline:

    E3_EXPOSURE_TOTAL
    E3_EXPOSURE_CERTAIN / POSSIBLE_ONLY / NO_CAUSAL_PATH
    rows of the interval carrying any NO_CAUSAL_PATH mass
    rows of the interval carrying ZERO CERTAIN mass          # a duration-level version
    first row at which NO_CAUSAL_PATH mass appears
    the same, split by arm (SELECTIVE / SHAM)

DECLARED IN ADVANCE, BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT EXISTS:

  * The SELECTIVE arm is expected to show almost no NO_CAUSAL_PATH mass, and that is NOT evidence
    that the endpoint is clean. It is the same degeneracy CLEA01 already established: after the
    intervention the daughter is the world's only Y source, so everything descends from it by
    construction. Any SELECTIVE number here is uninformative and must be labelled as such.
  * The informative arm is SHAM.
  * THE INCONVENIENT RESULT, NAMED NOW: if a substantial share of SHAM E3_EXPOSURE is
    NO_CAUSAL_PATH, then E3_EXPOSURE is not measuring the daughter's persistence in the control arm,
    the SELECTIVE-vs-SHAM contrast that OMLDCT02 was built on is partly a contrast between
    different things, and every successor design that reuses this endpoint inherits the problem.
    That result will be reported in those words if it occurs.
  * NO THRESHOLD separates "substantial" from "negligible". The distribution is published and the
    reader decides. No gate in this mission turns on a magnitude.

======================================================================
5. THE SIZING INSTRUMENT
======================================================================

Turn the accrual arithmetic into committed, self-tested code that any future campaign must pass
through. Required behaviour:

    input   a pair target, a confidence level, and an empirical ledger of (cost, admissible)
    output  the arm-instance ceiling that attains that confidence, by bootstrap over the ledger
    output  P(reaching the target) at any proposed ceiling
    refuse  to return a ceiling below the target's own p50 cost without saying so

It must reproduce, from OMLDCT02_SEALED_LEDGER.jsonl:

    realised admissible rate            33/805
    cost per admissible pair            15.47 arm-instances
    P(41 pairs | ceiling 512)           ~0.076
    ceiling for P(41 pairs) >= 0.95     ~800

and must carry a self-test that fails if any of those moves. It records, as a fixed fact and not as
an opinion, that the TLMR01 developmental sizing rate 22/256 is not exchangeable with the realised
rate: expected 69.2 admissible in 805 seeds, observed 33.

These are sampling-yield quantities. They are NOT the paired duration/exposure endpoints and they do
not touch the owner's rule that fewer than 41 pairs may not be interpreted using the paired
p-values. That rule is untouched and restated in the final report.
