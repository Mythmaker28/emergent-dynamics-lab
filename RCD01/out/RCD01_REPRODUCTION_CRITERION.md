# RCD01 §9 — LE CRITERE MINIMAL DE REPRODUCTION

```
MINIMAL_REPRODUCTION       = R0 et R1 et R2
STRONG_SELF_REPRODUCTION   = R0 et R1 et R2 et R3
HEREDITY                   = critere separe, hors des deux
```

## Les questions auxquelles un critere doit repondre

**what exactly is the parent system** — the spatial centre, under the frozen toroidal single-linkage partition of Y-occupied cells, whose centroid is closest to the single pre-separation centroid at the separation step. It is an ORGANISATION, not a particle: no founder identity is required.

**what exactly is the daughter system** — the other component at the separation step, tracked forward by maximum cell overlap then minimum toroidal centroid distance

**when is the daughter born** — at the separation step t0, the first step at which the frozen classifier returns exactly two spatial centres

**when is it mature** — at t0 + T_PRIMARY_STEPS - 1 = t0 + 249, the frozen FDFLT01 maturation event, T_PRIMARY_STEPS being ceil of one e-folding of the slowest mode of the X operator

**what makes it independent** — its qualified local X response survives removal of the parent centre for a physically derived hold interval; nothing weaker is causal

**what material turnover is required** — its local X mass at maturation must exceed the exact 95% binomial upper bound on the number of pre-separation molecules surviving anywhere in the world; no percentage is chosen

**does the parent need to survive** — no. The criterion is about the daughter's independence from the parent, not the parent's persistence. Requiring parent survival would import the founder-survival gate that FLCR01 rejected.

**does the daughter need reproductive competence** — not for the MINIMAL claim. Requiring it would make the definition regress infinitely. It is the STRONG claim.

Aucune genealogie de particule n'est employee : organisation-level identity is sufficient at every step of the criterion, and particle identity was already shown irrelevant by FLCR01.

## Exigence avant tout test

- a declared deterministic tie-break in the parent/daughter rule — RCD01 found one world of 192 where the two components are EXACTLY equidistant from the pre-separation centroid and the rule is silent
