# RCD01 §3 — MATRICE DES CRITERES DE REPRODUCTION

## Formulation candidate, attaquee

a functional organising system generates a spatially distinct second organising system of the same operational class, using newly maintained material, and the second system can persist functionally without depending on the continued functioning of the original.

**Attaque —** 'of the same operational class' is undefined  
**Reponse —** it is defined operationally here as satisfying the same frozen functional maturation criterion the first centre satisfies: a qualified local X response held for T_primary steps. No new predicate is introduced.

**Attaque —** 'newly maintained material' invites an arbitrary percentage  
**Reponse —** it is replaced by a threshold-free comparison against the certified upper bound on surviving pre-separation material, derived from muX alone.

**Attaque —** 'without depending on the continued functioning of the original' is causal and cannot be read off passive data  
**Reponse —** correct, and this is the decisive limitation. It is why R2 is separated out and why RCD01 does not claim it.

**Attaque —** the formulation says nothing about generations  
**Reponse —** deliberate. Generation competence is R3 and is argued to be beyond the MINIMUM first-generation criterion.

**Attaque —** the formulation could be satisfied by a fission of one cloud into two halves  
**Reponse —** that is exactly what R1 excludes: a halved cloud is old material, and the certified bound rejects it.

### Preuve par niveau

- **R0 (FUNCTIONAL_MULTIPLICATION)** — 53 of 192 fresh worlds; the FDFLT01 qualified claim

- **R1 (MATERIAL_RECONSTRUCTION)** — 25 of 53 R0 worlds satisfy the threshold-free bound; the count is a lower bound because the bound credits the daughter with every surviving old molecule in the world

- **R2 (FUNCTIONAL_INDEPENDENCE)** — no world in any dataset had one centre removed while another persisted; the only executable intervention zeroes the entire Y field

- **R3 (REPRODUCTIVE_COMPETENCE)** — 25 worlds reach a third centre and 20 of them had two functional centres first, but the frozen stop rule ends every world at the third centre, so 0 of 25 have enough record left for the third centre to mature

## Hierarchie retenue

- `MINIMAL_REPRODUCTION = R0 and R1 and R2`
- `STRONG_SELF_REPRODUCTION = R0 and R1 and R2 and R3`
- `HEREDITY = a separate future criterion, not part of either`

## Alternatives examinees et rejetees

- **MINIMAL_REPRODUCTION = R0 + R1 only** — a second centre that dies the moment the first is removed was never an independent system; without R2 the word reproduction carries no causal content.
- **MINIMAL_REPRODUCTION = R0 + R2 only** — a cloud that splits in two and both halves persist would qualify, which is division of substance, not production of new substance.
- **MINIMAL_REPRODUCTION must include R3** — requiring the daughter to reproduce before the first event counts as reproduction is an infinite regress; R3 is the right criterion for a STRONG claim, not the minimum.
- **require survival of the original founder particle** — already rejected by FLCR01; particle identity is not organisational identity.
